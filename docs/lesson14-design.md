# Approved Lesson 14 design constraints

This note records the approved instructional design refinements. Experiment 1 is implemented as Instructional Milestone 2; Experiments 2–5 remain planned.

## Multipole visualization

The common comparison scale will be the **signed contribution to electric potential at the current probe**:

\[V_n(r,\theta)=\frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\frac{Q_n}{r^{n+1}}P_n(\cos\theta).\]

Moments \(Q_n\) have different units (C·m\(^n\)) and must not be plotted as comparable bars. A future readout may show an individual moment with its correct units, but the spatial comparison will use \(V_n\) in volts. Full potential, truncated series, and numerical reference calculations will be identified separately.

## Experiment 1: a movable coordinate origin

The physical charge remains fixed. The student can move the origin or observation probe at any time. When only the origin moves along the lesson's symmetry axis, the charge and probe stay fixed in world space. The reference axis, vectors \(\mathbf r'\) and \(\mathbf r\), and exterior convergence boundary \(R_{\max}\) update while exact physical potential remains fixed. When the probe moves, its exact potential generally changes. The exterior series is shown only where \(r>R_{\max}\).

The revised station has two modes sharing the same physics: Guided Experiment offers short peripheral prompts and an optional prediction control; Free Exploration hides prompts. No phase gates movement. Vectors appear only when the origin or probe is selected, with their labels kept below the apparatus. Mathematical readouts appear after selection: the origin view shows moments with proper units; the probe view compares potential contributions in volts. The exact Coulomb reading stays separate from the exterior sum, which is withheld inside the convergence sphere. Subsequent experiments should retain this ungated physical exploration.
